UltraRogue is a dungeon game in the line of Rogue, written by Herb
Chong. You choose one of ten character classes, from fighter to ninja,
and go down through fifty levels of rooms, mazes and throne rooms, past
trading posts and monsters that cast spells and summon others, to bring
back the Amulet of Yendor and the other artifacts.

This is UltraRogue 1.06 Alpha, dated December 21, 1995.

What is different from 1.05:

- The dungeon is fifty levels deep instead of a hundred, and the
  artifacts are found at half the depth. Monsters get stronger sooner,
  there are more of them and more traps, and fewer things to find.
- Trading posts come only at the start of the game or through an
  entrance found on a level.
- The hero is weaker: hit points and spell points come back more
  slowly, he carries less, levels need more experience, every ring
  costs food and so do spells.
- Familiars keep up with their master; a monster may join one of its
  own kind that attacks it. Rangers and paladins can summon familiars.
- Things can be dropped where a monster or the hero stands, and charged
  weapons can be zapped again.
- Food lasts longer on a bigger screen.
- The control keys work again, the top ten is laid out in columns, and a
  winning ninja is made Master of the Wind.
- The source is run through indent(1), and a checked memory allocator
  watches the game's use of storage.
